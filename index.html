What Is Manifest Trade?

Manifest Trade is a decentralized trading platform designed for users who want to interact with digital asset markets through blockchain-based infrastructure. Instead of depending entirely on a traditional centralized exchange, the platform is built around on-chain market activity and wallet-based access.

The platform focuses on bringing an orderbook trading experience to the Solana ecosystem. This model allows traders to view buy and sell orders, consider available liquidity, and submit trading instructions through a decentralized interface.

Manifest Trade also explores liquidity strategies, vault-based products, and options functionality. These tools are intended to support different types of market participants, including active traders, liquidity providers, and users interested in structured digital asset products.

How the Manifest Trade Orderbook Works

An orderbook displays open buy and sell instructions for a market. Buy orders are commonly called bids, while sell orders are called asks. The difference between the best available bid and ask is known as the spread.

Traders may use market orders when they want execution near the current available price, or limit orders when they want to specify the price at which they are willing to trade. The actual execution price can depend on liquidity, order size, market movement, and matching conditions.

  • Review the available bids and asks.
  • Choose a supported market and trading pair.
  • Select an order type and enter the desired amount.
  • Review estimated costs and transaction details.
  • Approve the transaction through the connected wallet.
Important: On-chain transactions may involve network fees, price impact, slippage, and execution delays. Always review the final transaction details before signing.

Spot Markets and Token Trading

Spot trading involves exchanging one digital asset for another without creating a future-dated derivative position. On Manifest Trade, users may explore supported spot markets and use the available orderbook to manage their trading instructions.

The quality of a spot market can depend on trading volume, orderbook depth, the number of active participants, and the spread between buyers and sellers. A market with limited liquidity may experience larger price movements when larger orders are submitted.

Options Trading Concepts

Options are financial instruments that provide rights connected to an underlying asset. A call option generally relates to the right to buy at a specified strike price, while a put option generally relates to the right to sell at a specified strike price. Options may also include an expiration date and collateral requirements.
